# Meta Platforms Q2 2025 Financial Results

## Performance Overview
Meta Platforms, Inc. (META) reported an excellent second quarter for 2025. Total revenue was $41.8 billion, up 18% year-over-year. The company's focus on efficiency and AI-driven ad targeting resulted in a net income of $15.2 billion, yielding an operating margin of 41%.

## Family of Apps
The Family of Apps (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger) generated $41.2 billion in revenue. Ad impressions delivered across the family of apps increased by 14%, while the average price per ad grew by 4%. Daily Active People (DAP) reached a new milestone of 3.45 billion. Reels engagement continues to grow, capturing market share in short-form video.

## Reality Labs
Reality Labs, Meta's division focused on the metaverse and AR/VR, reported $600 million in revenue, primarily from Quest 3 headset sales. However, the division posted an operating loss of $4.1 billion. Mark Zuckerberg defended the investment, emphasizing that mixed reality and smart glasses (like the Ray-Ban Meta glasses) are foundational to the next computing platform.

## AI and Open Source
Meta highlighted the success of its open-source Llama 4 models, which have seen millions of downloads. AI integration across its advertising platforms has automated ad creation for millions of small businesses, significantly increasing return on ad spend (ROAS) for advertisers.

## Outlook
Meta expects Q3 2025 total revenue to be in the range of $43 billion to $45.5 billion. Total expenses for 2025 are expected to be between $96 billion and $99 billion, reflecting infrastructure investments to support AI research and product development.
