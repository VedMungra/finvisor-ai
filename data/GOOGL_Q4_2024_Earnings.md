# Alphabet Q4 2024 Earnings Report

## Overview
Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) reported Q4 2024 financial results, demonstrating robust performance in its core Search business and accelerating growth in Google Cloud. Total revenue was $85.3 billion, an increase of 13% compared to the same quarter last year. 

## Google Services
Google Search and other advertising revenues were $46.5 billion, up 11%. YouTube ads generated $9.2 billion, reflecting strong engagement in Shorts. Overall Google Services revenue was $73.4 billion, supported by the integration of Gemini AI features directly into search results, which improved user engagement metrics.

## Google Cloud
Google Cloud revenue reached $9.8 billion, a 26% year-over-year increase. Operating income for the cloud division expanded to $950 million, proving that the business segment is not only scaling but becoming increasingly profitable. AI solutions contributed significantly to new enterprise deals.

## Capital Returns and Investments
The company announced an expanded share repurchase program of up to $50 billion. Capital expenditures were $10.5 billion for the quarter, primarily reflecting investments in technical infrastructure, particularly servers and data centers to train and deploy state-of-the-art AI models.

## Future Outlook
Sundar Pichai noted that Alphabet is fundamentally an AI-first company. The focus for 2025 will be on monetizing generative AI tools across Google Workspace and driving further efficiencies in Search using the Gemini model family.
