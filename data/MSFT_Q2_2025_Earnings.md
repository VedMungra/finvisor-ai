# Microsoft Q2 2025 Earnings Report

## Executive Summary
Microsoft (MSFT) announced Q2 2025 earnings showing strong momentum in its cloud and AI segments. Total revenue for the quarter was $68.2 billion, representing a 14% year-over-year increase. Net income reached $24.5 billion, up 22%. 

## Intelligent Cloud
The Intelligent Cloud segment generated $28.5 billion in revenue, growing by 21%. Azure revenue grew 28% year-over-year, largely driven by enterprise adoption of AI services and OpenAI models hosted on Azure. 

## Productivity and Business Processes
Revenue in this segment was $21.3 billion, a 15% increase. Office 365 Commercial revenue grew by 16%, driven by seat growth and higher revenue per user as companies adopted Microsoft 365 Copilot subscriptions. LinkedIn revenue increased by 11%.

## More Personal Computing
More Personal Computing saw a modest growth of 5%, bringing in $18.4 billion. Windows OEM revenue remained relatively flat, but Xbox content and services revenue grew 32% following the integration of recent studio acquisitions.

## Forward Guidance
CEO Satya Nadella emphasized that the company is transitioning from talking about AI to scaling AI. Microsoft expects Q3 revenue to be in the range of $69 billion to $70 billion, with continued high capital expenditures to support AI infrastructure and datacenter expansion globally.
