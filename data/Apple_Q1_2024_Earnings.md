# Apple Q1 2024 Earnings Report

**Date:** February 1, 2024

## Financial Highlights
- **Total Net Sales:** Apple announced financial results for its fiscal 2024 first quarter ended December 30, 2023. The Company posted quarterly revenue of $119.6 billion, up 2 percent year over year.
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Quarterly earnings per diluted share were $2.18, up 16 percent year over year.
- **Services Revenue:** Services revenue reached an all-time high of $23.1 billion, an increase of 11% compared to the prior-year quarter.
- **iPhone Revenue:** iPhone revenue was $69.7 billion, up 6% from the prior year.
- **Mac Revenue:** Mac revenue came in at $7.78 billion, slightly up from $7.74 billion in the prior-year quarter.
- **iPad Revenue:** iPad revenue declined significantly to $7.02 billion from $9.40 billion in the prior-year quarter.

## CEO Commentary
"Today Apple is reporting revenue growth for the December quarter fueled by iPhone sales, and an all-time revenue record in Services," said Tim Cook, Apple's CEO. "We are pleased to announce that our installed base of active devices has now surpassed 2.2 billion, reaching an all-time high across all products and geographic segments. And as customers begin experiencing the incredible Apple Vision Pro tomorrow, we are committed as ever to the pursuit of groundbreaking innovation."

## CFO Commentary
"Our top-line performance drove operating cash flow of nearly $40 billion, and allowed us to return almost $27 billion to our shareholders during the quarter," said Luca Maestri, Apple's CFO. "We are confident in our future, and continue to make significant investments across our business to support our long-term growth plans."

## Regional Performance
- **Americas:** $50.43 billion (up from $49.28 billion)
- **Europe:** $30.40 billion (up from $27.68 billion)
- **Greater China:** $20.82 billion (down from $23.90 billion)
- **Japan:** $7.77 billion (up from $6.75 billion)
- **Rest of Asia Pacific:** $10.16 billion (up from $9.54 billion)

## Capital Return Program
Apple's board of directors has declared a cash dividend of $0.24 per share of the Company's common stock. The dividend is payable on February 15, 2024, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on February 12, 2024.
