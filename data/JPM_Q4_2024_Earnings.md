# JPMorgan Chase Q4 2024 Earnings

## Financial Results
JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM) reported strong fourth-quarter 2024 results, demonstrating resilience in a shifting macroeconomic environment. Net revenue was $41.5 billion, and net income was $10.3 billion, or $3.45 per share. Full-year net income reached a record $52.1 billion.

## Consumer & Community Banking (CCB)
CCB revenue was $18.2 billion, relatively flat compared to the previous year. Average deposits decreased by 4% as customers sought higher-yielding alternatives, but card services revenue grew by 6% due to strong consumer spending volumes.

## Corporate & Investment Bank (CIB)
The CIB division saw a resurgence in activity. Revenue was $11.8 billion, up 15%. Investment banking revenue jumped 25%, driven by a recovery in debt underwriting and M&A advisory fees. Markets revenue remained stable, with fixed income slightly outperforming equities.

## Commercial Banking and Wealth Management
Commercial Banking reported revenue of $3.9 billion. Asset & Wealth Management revenue grew to $5.2 billion, with assets under management (AUM) rising to $3.5 trillion, supported by positive market performance and net inflows.

## Macroeconomic Outlook
CEO Jamie Dimon expressed cautious optimism regarding the US economy, noting that consumer balance sheets remain generally healthy despite inflation pressures. However, he warned of ongoing geopolitical risks and the unpredictable impacts of quantitative tightening. The bank increased its credit reserves slightly in anticipation of potential commercial real estate stress.
