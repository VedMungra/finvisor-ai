# Tesla, Inc. Q4 and Full Year 2025 Financial Results

## Executive Summary
AUSTIN, Texas, January 28, 2026 – Tesla, Inc. today announced its financial results for the fourth quarter and full year ended December 31, 2025. Q4 was a record-breaking quarter for both production and deliveries, driven by the continued scaling of the Cybertruck and the introduction of the refreshed Model Y. Total revenue for the quarter grew 14% year-over-year to $28.52 billion.

## Q4 2025 Financial Highlights
- **Total Revenue**: $28.52 billion, up 14% YoY.
- **Automotive Revenues**: $23.1 billion, an 11% increase YoY.
- **Energy Generation and Storage Revenue**: $3.2 billion, up 42% YoY, reflecting massive deployments of Megapack.
- **Services and Other Revenue**: $2.22 billion.
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**: $23.33 billion.
- **Gross Margin**: To calculate gross margin, use the formula: (Total Revenue - COGS) / Total Revenue.
- **Operating Expenses**: $2.65 billion, primarily due to intense R&D investments in artificial intelligence and the Dojo supercomputer.
- **Net Income (GAAP)**: $3.1 billion.
- **Free Cash Flow**: $2.4 billion for the quarter.

## Operational Milestones
### Vehicle Production & Deliveries
In Q4, we produced 525,000 vehicles and delivered 518,000 vehicles. For the full year 2025, vehicle deliveries totaled 1.95 million, narrowly missing the 2.0 million stretch goal but representing solid 12% growth over 2024. 
- Model 3/Y deliveries: 480,000
- Other models (S/X/Cybertruck): 38,000

### Energy Storage
Energy storage deployments reached an all-time high of 5.8 GWh in Q4 2025. The Lathrop Megafactory is now operating at full capacity, and the new Shanghai Megafactory has officially begun early production.

## Technology & Artificial Intelligence
### Full Self-Driving (FSD)
FSD version 13 has been rolled out to over 1.2 million customers in North America. The transition to end-to-end neural networks for both highway and city driving has reduced disengagement rates by 40% compared to version 12. Total cumulative miles driven on FSD Beta surpassed 1.5 billion miles in December.

### Optimus
The Optimus humanoid robot is now performing complex tasks in the Fremont factory, including battery cell sorting and autonomous navigation across dynamic factory floors. We expect limited external sales to select enterprise partners by late 2026.

## Management Commentary
"We are incredibly proud of the resilience our team showed in 2025," said Elon Musk, CEO. "Despite a challenging macroeconomic environment and high interest rates globally, we achieved record deliveries and massively expanded our energy storage business. Our investments in AI are beginning to compound, and we believe our compute infrastructure is now the most powerful in the world."

## Forward Outlook for 2026
For 2026, we anticipate vehicle volume growth to be notably lower than the growth rate achieved in 2025, as our teams work on the launch of the next-generation vehicle platform at Gigafactory Texas. However, we expect the Energy Generation and Storage business revenue growth rate to significantly outpace the Automotive business. Capital expenditures for 2026 are expected to exceed $10 billion as we aggressively expand AI compute clusters.
