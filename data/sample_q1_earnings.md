# GlobalTech Corp

## Q1_2026_Fiscal_Quarter

### Financial Results
GlobalTech Corp today announced financial results for its first quarter ended March 31, 2026.
Total revenue was $12.4 billion, an increase of 15% year-over-year. The growth was primarily driven by the strong adoption of our enterprise AI solutions and increased cloud infrastructure utilization.
Net income for the quarter was $3.1 billion, or $4.25 per diluted share, compared to $2.4 billion, or $3.15 per diluted share, in the same period last year.
Operating expenses increased by 8% to $4.2 billion, reflecting continued investments in research and development for our next-generation machine learning models.

### Product Releases
During Q1 2026, GlobalTech Corp successfully launched 'OmniMind Enterprise', our flagship generative AI platform tailored for the financial sector. 
OmniMind Enterprise features a context window of 2 million tokens and integrates directly with existing legacy banking systems. Early adoption has exceeded expectations, with over 50 Fortune 500 banks signing enterprise agreements.
Additionally, we released version 4.0 of our CloudSecure framework, introducing zero-trust architecture by default for all new deployments.

### Forward Looking Statements
For the second quarter of 2026, GlobalTech expects revenue to be between $12.8 billion and $13.2 billion. 
Operating margins are projected to remain stable at around 28%. We remain committed to returning value to shareholders through our ongoing $5 billion share repurchase program.
