# GlobalTech Q2 2026 Earnings Report

## Executive Summary
GlobalTech had an incredible second quarter, far exceeding Q1 numbers.

## Financial Results
- **Q2 Revenue**: $18.5 billion (a massive increase from Q1)
- **Operating Expenses**: $6.1 billion
- **Net Profit Margin**: To calculate this, subtract operating expenses from revenue, then divide by revenue. 
- **Stock Buyback**: The board authorized a new $2 billion stock repurchase program.

## Forward Outlook
We expect Q3 revenue to stabilize around $17 billion as we enter the slower holiday preparation season.
