# Amazon Q3 2024 Earnings Summary

## Financial Highlights
Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2024. Net sales increased 12% to $155.8 billion, compared with $138.9 billion in the third quarter of 2023. Operating income increased significantly to $13.5 billion, up from $8.6 billion a year ago.

## Amazon Web Services (AWS)
AWS remains a critical driver of profitability. AWS segment sales increased 18% year-over-year to $26.4 billion. Operating income for AWS was $9.8 billion. The company highlighted the rapid adoption of Amazon Bedrock, its generative AI service, which allows enterprise customers to build and scale AI applications securely.

## North America and International Retail
North America segment sales increased 10% to $93.6 billion, with operating income returning to a healthy $4.5 billion due to regionalization of the fulfillment network and reduced cost to serve. The International segment reported sales of $35.8 billion, growing 11%, with operating losses narrowing to $100 million.

## Advertising Services
Amazon's advertising business continued its rapid expansion, growing 21% to $13.9 billion. This was driven by sponsored product ads and new ad formats rolling out across Prime Video programming.

## CEO Commentary
Andy Jassy noted that Amazon is focused on integrating AI into every customer and seller experience. The upcoming holiday season is expected to break previous sales records, with Q4 revenue guidance placed between $170 billion and $180 billion.
