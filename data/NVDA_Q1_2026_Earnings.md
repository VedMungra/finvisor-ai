# NVIDIA Q1 2026 Earnings Report

## Phenomenal Growth Continues
NVIDIA (NVDA) reported record-breaking revenue for Q1 2026, driven by an insatiable global demand for generative AI training and inference. Total revenue came in at $28.0 billion, a staggering 250% increase year-over-year. Net income was $16.8 billion.

## Data Center Segment
The Data Center segment was the primary growth engine, reporting $23.5 billion in revenue. This growth was fueled by massive shipments of the new Blackwell architecture GPUs (B100 and B200) to hyperscalers, cloud providers, and sovereign AI initiatives. Networking revenue, led by InfiniBand solutions, also tripled.

## Gaming and Professional Visualization
Gaming revenue was $3.1 billion, reflecting stable demand for RTX 40-series and 50-series GPUs. Professional Visualization revenue hit $550 million, supported by enterprise adoption of NVIDIA Omniverse for industrial digitalization and digital twin simulations.

## Automotive and Robotics
Automotive revenue reached $380 million. The company announced several new partnerships with global automakers to integrate the DRIVE Thor platform for autonomous driving capabilities. Additionally, the Isaac robotics platform saw significant uptake among logistics and manufacturing firms.

## Guidance
Jensen Huang stated that "the next industrial revolution has begun." The company projects Q2 2026 revenue of approximately $30.5 billion. Supply chain constraints are easing, but demand for Blackwell chips continues to outpace supply well into next year.
